Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Drama Shorts Automation ("the App") handles information when it is used to publish and manage content for the AI Drama Shorts Facebook Page.

1. Information the App Uses

The App may use information provided by Meta's APIs that is necessary to operate the Facebook Page publishing workflow, such as Page identification, Page access information, and content being published to the Page.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Meta's APIs is used only to operate the authorized Page-management and content-publishing functions of the App. The App is not intended to sell personal information or use it for unrelated advertising purposes.

3. Facebook and Meta Data

The App uses Meta platform APIs and is subject to applicable Meta platform terms and policies. Information received from Meta is handled according to the permissions granted to the App and the purposes described above.

4. Data Storage

The automation may store configuration information and operational logs needed to run the publishing workflow. Access credentials and tokens should be stored securely and should not be shared publicly.

5. Data Sharing

The App does not intentionally sell or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be transmitted to Meta as necessary to perform actions requested through Meta's APIs.

6. Data Deletion

If you want information associated with the App removed, contact the administrator using the contact information below. You may also revoke the App's access through the relevant Meta/Facebook settings.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect App configuration and access credentials. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality changes. The updated version will be posted at this location with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the administrator at the email address associated with the AI Drama Shorts Automation app.
